Diabetes Companion

Rafiki wa Kisukari

A free app that helps people with diabetes log blood sugar, understand their numbers and learn to live well – made for Africa, in the languages people speak.

Coming soon to Google Play

Simple logbook

Blood sugar, insulin and tablets, meals, activity, weight, HbA1c, blood pressure and lab tests – in a few taps.

Clear advice

After each reading the app explains what to do now, with clear warnings for low and very high blood sugar.

Report for the doctor

A PDF report with time in range, patterns through the day and insulin and carbohydrates per day.

Learn

48 short articles based on WHO, the American Diabetes Association and large clinical studies.

Local food

A carbohydrate table with ugali, chapati, fufu, injera, jollof, cassava, arepa and many more.

Private

Everything stays on your phone. No account, no data sent to us.
